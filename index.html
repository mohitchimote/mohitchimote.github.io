About

Domain depth meets hands-on engineering.

I joined TCS in 2012 as a graduate and have spent 13 years across programme delivery, implementation, presales, and product management — all in financial services, and predominantly in UK mortgage lending. I know this domain properly: the regulatory landscape, the lender economics, the broker relationships, the processing bottlenecks, and where technology can genuinely move the needle.

Some are live with real users. Some are proof-of-concepts exploring what's feasible.

I hold a BEng in Computer Science & Engineering, a PG Diploma in Management from Welingkar, and am Microsoft Azure certified. I'm based in Grays, Essex and work across London.

01 · AI · FinTech
AI Summariser
Proof of Concept
Local AI summarisation and document verification microservice for financial services. Two backends: Python/FastAPI with llama-cpp and Metal GPU acceleration (Gemma 4 GGUF), and a Node.js/Ollama service with PDF export. Runs entirely offline — built for regulated environments where data can't leave the network. Domain-specific prompt types cover mortgage applications, valuation reports, offer conditions, and payslip verification. PoC stage; production path depends on use-case sign-off.
